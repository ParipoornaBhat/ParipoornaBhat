## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/LnQjpWaON8nhr21vNW/giphy.gif" width="32" /> How I work

<details open>
<summary><b>My engineering principles</b></summary>
<br/>

- **TypeScript end to end** — type safety across the wire matters more to me than any single framework.
- **Backend:** Hono or Next.js route handlers; **Drizzle** for new builds, **Prisma** where the schema already exists.
- **Monorepos:** Turborepo + pnpm, Dockerised, deployed on Vercel or self-hosted.
- **Auth & access control first** — RBAC designed in from day one, never bolted on afterwards.
- **Ship, measure, refine** — rapid prototyping with AI in the loop, always with a clear picture of what I want built and why.

</details>
